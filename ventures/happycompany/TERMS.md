# Happy Company: licence terms that protect the people measured

These clauses are part of the licence every customer accepts. They are here,
in the product's own repository, because the promise they make is enforced
in the product's code as well as on paper.

## 1. Use ban

The grade, every indicator, every figure and every report produced by
Happy Company describe **working conditions for a team**. The customer shall
not use them, alone or combined with other data, to assess, rank, pay,
reward, promote, discipline or dismiss any person, or as an input to any
such decision. This includes bonus and variable-pay schemes, performance
reviews, calibration sessions and redundancy selection.

A team's grade is not a mark on its manager either. It may be used to
decide where support, staffing or planning changes are needed.

## 2. No individual data

The customer shall not attempt to re-identify a person from Happy Company
output, for example by comparing overlapping teams, adjusting team
membership, or combining the output with other monitoring data.

## 3. Information and participation

Before activation, the customer informs the people whose activity is
counted, using the notice in `WORKS_COUNCIL.md` §9 or its equivalent, and
consults the works council, staff representatives or health and safety
committee where the law or a collective agreement requires it. The "What we
measure" tab in the app is available to every member of every team.

## 4. Breach

Use in breach of clause 1 or 2 ends the licence for the site concerned. The
vendor will not assist any use in breach and will say so publicly if asked.

## 5. Not a medical or diagnostic tool

Happy Company does not detect or diagnose stress, burnout, mental health or
any other condition of any person. It describes the conditions ISO 45003
lists as psychosocial hazards, at team level, from work metadata.
