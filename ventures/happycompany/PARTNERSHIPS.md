# Happy Company: partnerships to pursue

Distribution for a compliance-shaped product comes from the people who
already sit in the compliance conversation. None of these has been
contacted; each entry says why it fits, what to offer, and what to check
first. Names and programmes are from public information and must be
verified before outreach.

## 1. Training arms of ISO certification bodies

TÜV SÜD, TÜV Rheinland, DEKRA, BSI, SGS, Bureau Veritas, DNV and LRQA certify
ISO 45001 and run academies that train internal auditors and OH&S managers.

- **The constraint.** Accreditation rules (ISO/IEC 17021-1) forbid a
  certification body from consulting for or recommending tools to the
  organisations it certifies. The certification side will not partner; the
  training side can use the app as a teaching example of psychosocial
  hazard screening.
- **Offer.** A free training licence and a sample evidence pack for their
  ISO 45003 and ISO 45001 internal-auditor courses; a joint webinar on
  "evidence for psychosocial risk under ISO 45001" ahead of the revision.
- **Check first.** Whether the academy is a separate legal entity, and its
  rules on naming commercial tools in course material.

## 2. German statutory health and accident insurers

- **Health insurers (Krankenkassen)** fund workplace health promotion under
  §20b SGB V and run regional coordination offices for employers
  (BGF-Koordinierungsstellen). Psychosocial risk is a named priority of the
  national prevention strategy.
- **Accident insurers (Berufsgenossenschaften)**, notably VBG for IT and
  service companies, advise members on the psychosocial risk assessment
  required by §5 ArbSchG and GDA.
- **Offer.** Happy Company as the screening step in a funded workplace
  health programme: the insurer's consultant runs the workshop, the app
  supplies the team figures and the evidence pack. The works-council pack
  answers the co-determination question they always get.
- **Check first.** Whether a funded programme may name a paid tool, and
  whether the insurer requires its own survey instrument alongside.

## 3. Occupational health providers and EAPs

Occupational health services (in Germany, providers such as B·A·D) and
employee assistance programmes already sell psychosocial risk assessment
and counselling. The app gives them a continuous team-level signal between
their annual assessments; they give the app's "act now" teams somewhere to
go. Revenue share or reseller terms; the app itself never refers a person.

## 4. Atlassian Solution Partners

Partners who implement Jira Service Management and Confluence for HR and
compliance teams can bundle the app with a works-agreement workshop.
Marketplace partner programme terms apply.

## 5. Certifications the customer pursues

Top Employers, Great Place to Work and B Corp do not endorse tools. What
helps is that the evidence pack and the disclosure drafts
(`src/lib/disclosures.mjs`) make the customer's own submission easier. Say
exactly that, and never imply an endorsement.

## What never to do in a partnership

Sell or share customer data (there is none to share: the app has no
egress), let a partner see an individual, rank customers, or let a partner
describe a Happy Company level as a certification.
