Jet lag: what works and what doesn't
Light is the main lever
Timed light and darkness move your body clock. Everything else in the plan helps, but less.
East is harder than west
Your clock stays up later more easily than it goes to bed earlier, so flying east takes longer to get over.
Dark sunglasses beat blue-blockers
All visible light reaches your clock. The darker and more wrap-around the pair, the better.
More melatonin isn't better
A low dose of a fast-release tablet at the right time works; a big or slow-release dose lingers into the morning.
Water doesn't cure jet lag
Drink normally on the plane for comfort, but it won't move your clock.
Jet lag diets haven't held up
Eating on local time helps; special fasting plans have little evidence behind them.
Alcohol backfires
It helps you fall asleep, then breaks up the second half of the night.
