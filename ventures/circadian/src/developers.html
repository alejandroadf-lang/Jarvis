Circadian API

A jet lag plan for any flight, from one request.

Send the flights. Get back when to sleep, when to seek and avoid light, the last coffee and melatonin, day by day, on each city's own clock, with how many days the body clock needs to catch up.

What it handles

  • Connections, round trips, open jaws and multi-city itineraries, each journey starting from where the last one left the body clock.
  • East and west at their own rates (about 1 h a day east, 1.5 h west), and the long way round when it is quicker.
  • Trips under three days: stay on home time instead of shifting twice.
  • The traveller's own sleep times or chronotype, pre-flight shifting of up to three days, light glasses or a light box.
  • Every plan carries a plain-language summary and the disclaimer: it is general guidance, not medical advice.

Quickstart

curl -X POST BASE/v2/plan \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ca_live_..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"departure":"2026-10-20T00:30","departure_tz":"Asia/Bangkok",
       "arrival":"2026-10-20T07:15","arrival_tz":"Europe/London"}'

A plan comes back in tens of milliseconds. Times are local wall-clock times with IANA zones. Connections go in legs, round trips and multi-city trips in journeys. Errors are always {"error": {"code", "message"}}. The full schema is in openapi.json, which imports into Postman or Insomnia. Every answer carries X-Quota-Remaining, and GET /v1/usage tells you the month so far.
